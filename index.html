About Mega

Three decades powering Australia's toughest builds.

Founded by Kevin Hull in 1991, Mega Electrics has grown from a Brisbane contractor into a four-branch operation trusted by tier-one builders, ports, utilities and miners.

  • HV, LV, fibre and switchboards under one contract, so nothing falls between trades.
  • Our own cable trailers, HV test sets and workshop, so jobs run on your schedule.
  • Licensed in Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria.

How we work

From first call to switch-on.

One team carries the job from scope to support, so the people who plan it are the people who power it up.

Scope

Build

Our electricians, jointers and workshop deliver the installation with our own plant.

Test

HV, LV and fibre are tested and commissioned in-house, with full reports.
