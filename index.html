From empty yard to energised

How a substation comes alive.

  1. TrenchExcavators open the cable route, with a spotter on every machine and barriers round every trench.
  2. CableBlack HV cable comes off the drum on jacks, over rollers and into the trench, with a drum tender on the brake.
  3. JointOur jointers make and terminate every HV joint in a clean, dry joint bay.
  4. TestInsulation and VLF tests on every cable, every result recorded and handed over.
  5. EnergiseIsolation released, switching done by the book. The substation is live and the project has power.
