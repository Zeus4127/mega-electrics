Privacy

This website and the Mega Electrics app do not track you and do not collect personal data on their own.

If you send a quote request, the details you type (name, company, phone, email and the job) go only to Mega Electrics and are used only to reply to you. They are not sold or shared.

The app has no ads and no tracking. Tapping a phone number, email address or map link opens your phone's own app.

Questions: call 1300 090 797 or email info@megaelectrics.com.au.
