Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026. This is a preview of the new Mega Electrics website and app.

What we collect

This website and the Mega Electrics app do not track you, use no advertising or tracking cookies, and do not collect personal data on their own.

If you send a quote request, report a fault, or apply for a job, the details you type (such as name, company, phone, email and the job) go only to Mega Electrics and are used only to reply to you and do the work. They are not sold or shared, except with people who need them to do that work.

What stays on your device

To keep working with a weak signal, your browser or the app keeps a saved copy of the pages. Display choices you make (such as text size) are saved on your device only. You can clear them in your browser or phone settings.

Automated decisions

We do not use computer programs to make decisions about you using your personal information. Messages may be sorted by topic so they reach the right team, but a person reads and decides on every request.

Phone, email and maps

The app has no ads and no tracking. Tapping a phone number, email address or map link opens your phone's own app.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint: call 1300 090 797 or email info@megaelectrics.com.au. If you are not happy with our reply, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
