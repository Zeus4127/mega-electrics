Rules

Do

  • One phone number everywhere: 1300 090 797.
  • Real Mega photos and video from Australian sites only, with correct PPE.
  • Plain Australian English, short sentences, numbers in real units (kV, km, t).
  • Mark unconfirmed facts as tbc until Mega confirms them.

Don't

  • No stock photos, cartoons or AI-made people (professional mode).
  • No colours outside the token list, no gradients except the orange swoosh and the navy bands.
  • No other phone numbers (07 3341 11697 is invalid).
  • No claims, ratings or reviews that aren't real and checked.
