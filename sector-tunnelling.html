What we do here

Our work in this sector

  • 22 kV and 11 kV supply for tunnel boring machines
  • Temporary substations and earth grids
  • Tunnel lighting, power and ventilation systems
  • Road headers, jumbos, dust boxes, fan and pump starters
  • Electrical and mechanical assembly and maintenance of TBMs
  • Shift cover around the clock while the drive runs
Where we have done itClem7, Airport Link and Legacy Way in Brisbane; M2 Tunnel, NorthConnex, Sydney Metro and M5 in Sydney; Metro Tunnel in Melbourne.
